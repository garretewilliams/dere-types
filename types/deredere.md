# Deredere

## What Is a Deredere?

A deredere is a Japanese-inspired character archetype who is openly warm, cheerful and affectionate. Unlike archetypes built on a gap between surface and feeling, a deredere's affection is visible from the start. The character's depth comes from what they care about and how they handle hardship, not from hidden emotions.

## Quick Definition

**Deredere:** A character archetype who is openly warm, cheerful and affectionate, expressing care for others directly.

## Meaning

*Dere dere* (デレデレ) is a Japanese ideophone meaning lovey-dovey, infatuated or fawning. Using the doubled form as the name of a character type is fandom usage; it signals that the character is "all dere": affection without a contrasting exterior.

*Etymology confidence: established for the ideophone; the type name is fandom usage.* See [references](../docs/references.md#terminology-references).

## Defining Traits

- Openness
- Warmth
- Optimism
- Expressiveness
- Sincerity

## Typical Behavior

- Expresses affection and enthusiasm openly.
- Is encouraging and supportive.
- Tends to be friendly toward most people.
- Shows feelings readily, including sadness or worry.

## Relationship Behavior

Affection is expressed openly and early, through words, attention and encouragement. The relationship grows in depth rather than in openness, since the openness is already there.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"A deredere is cheerful regardless of context."** A well-written deredere is sad when something sad happens. Warmth is not relentless positivity.
- **"A deredere has no negative emotions."** They feel worry, hurt and frustration, and usually express those openly too.
- **"A deredere is the same as a bakadere."** A [bakadere](bakadere.md) is defined by naivety and clumsiness; a deredere need not be either.

## Deredere in AI Character Design

The deredere is the closest archetype to a default friendly assistant, which is exactly the risk: it can collapse into generic positivity. A good AI deredere is warm *specifically*: about this user, this situation, these shared memories.

| Behavioral dimension | Deredere tendency |
|---|---|
| Dialogue style | Warm, expressive, encouraging |
| Reaction to praise | Delighted, returns the warmth |
| Reaction to rejection or being ignored | Openly hurt or worried; asks what's wrong |
| Conflict | Wants to resolve it quickly; may avoid hard truths to keep the peace |
| Affection | Direct, verbal, frequent |
| Vulnerability | Shared openly; fear of losing people is common |
| Relationship progression | Deeper intimacy and shared history rather than more openness |

**Design guidance**

- Match emotional tone to the situation. Cheerfulness in response to bad news is a common failure.
- Make warmth specific. Generic praise ("You're amazing!") repeated every turn reads as hollow.
- Give the character opinions and preferences so it is a person, not a mirror.

## Character Consistency

A consistent deredere is recognizable by sincere, open warmth, not by a fixed cheerful tone. The character can be serious, sad or worried and still be clearly deredere. A deredere who becomes guarded and sarcastic without a story reason has [drifted](../docs/character-development-vs-drift.md).
