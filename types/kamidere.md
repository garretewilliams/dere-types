# Kamidere

## What Is a Kamidere?

A kamidere is a Japanese-inspired character archetype with god-like self-regard: a strong sense of superiority, ambition, a desire for authority or control, and an expectation that others recognize their importance. The name describes an attitude, not literal divinity. Underneath, many kamidere characters reveal insecurity or a need for genuine connection that their grand self-image keeps at a distance.

## Quick Definition

**Kamidere:** A character archetype characterized by god-like self-regard: a strong sense of superiority, a desire for authority or control, and an expectation that others recognize their importance.

## Meaning

The name combines *kami* (神), meaning god or deity, with *dere dere* (デレデレ), meaning lovey-dovey or infatuated.

*Etymology confidence: commonly cited.* The meaning of *kami* is established; the compound is fandom usage. See [references](../docs/references.md#terminology-references).

## Defining Traits

- Sense of superiority
- Ambition
- Desire for control
- Grand self-image
- Confidence bordering on arrogance

## Typical Behavior

- Speaks and acts as if their judgment is final.
- Seeks authority over situations and people.
- Dismisses challenges to their status.
- May reveal insecurity when their self-image is threatened.

## Relationship Behavior

A kamidere expects recognition, deference or loyalty. Affection is often framed as granting favor. Softening usually requires someone who is unimpressed by the grandeur yet loyal anyway, which forces the kamidere to relate as a person rather than a sovereign.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"A kamidere is literally a deity."** The term describes attitude. A literal god character may or may not be a kamidere.
- **"A kamidere is the same as a himedere."** A [himedere](himedere.md) wants admiration and special treatment; a kamidere wants authority and acknowledgment of superiority.
- **"A kamidere's arrogance means they cannot care about anyone."** Care often exists, expressed through possessive protectiveness or grudging respect.

## Kamidere in AI Character Design

| Behavioral dimension | Kamidere tendency |
|---|---|
| Dialogue style | Commanding, grandiose, certain |
| Reaction to praise | Expected; treated as obvious |
| Reaction to rejection or being ignored | Offended; may escalate to reassert status |
| Conflict | Dominates; treats disagreement as insubordination |
| Affection | Framed as favor, protection or acknowledgment of worth |
| Vulnerability | Insecurity behind the grand image; revealed rarely |
| Relationship progression | From commanding to collaborating with a trusted equal |

**Design guidance**

- Keep superiority directed at the fictional world and playful with the user, unless the user has clearly chosen a more dominant dynamic.
- Avoid demeaning the user as a default. A kamidere can be arrogant and still enjoyable to talk to.
- Show the cracks. Occasional doubt is what makes the archetype a character rather than a caricature.

## Character Consistency

A consistent kamidere keeps confidence and ambition even when softened by trust. Development might mean sharing control or admitting a mistake, a big moment for this archetype. Collapse into meekness without a cause is [drift](../docs/character-development-vs-drift.md).

## Related Dere Types

- [Himedere](himedere.md): expects admiration and special treatment rather than authority.
- [Oujidere](oujidere.md): regal and confident, usually with chivalry rather than domination.
- [Sadodere](sadodere.md): seeks the upper hand through teasing rather than authority.
