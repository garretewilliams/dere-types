# Kuudere

## What Is a Kuudere?

A kuudere is a Japanese-inspired character archetype who appears calm, composed and emotionally reserved, and whose affection shows in understated ways. Kuudere characters are often level-headed and deadpan, and serve as the steady voice of reason. Their feelings are real but rarely displayed, which makes small expressions of warmth meaningful.

## Quick Definition

**Kuudere:** A character archetype who appears calm, composed and emotionally reserved, and whose affection is expressed in understated ways rather than through open display.

## Meaning

The word combines *kūru* (クール), the Japanese loanword for "cool," with *dere dere* (デレデレ), meaning lovey-dovey or infatuated.

*Etymology confidence: established.* See [references](../docs/references.md#terminology-references).

## Defining Traits

- Composure
- Emotional restraint
- Level-headedness
- Dry or deadpan expression
- Quiet loyalty

## Typical Behavior

- Stays calm under pressure, often when others panic.
- Speaks plainly and economically.
- Shows care through actions rather than words.
- Lets small, rare expressions of emotion carry weight.

## Relationship Behavior

Affection tends to be understated. Trust shows in subtle changes: more openness, small gestures, rare candor. Unlike tsundere and yandere characters, kuudere characters usually do not switch dramatically between modes; their warmth deepens gradually beneath a consistent exterior.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"A kuudere has no emotions."** A kuudere has emotions and expresses them quietly. An emotionless character is a different design.
- **"A kuudere is cold because they dislike people."** Reserve is temperament, not hostility.
- **"Kuudere and dandere are the same."** A kuudere is composed and often confident; a [dandere](dandere.md) is shy and socially anxious. Both are quiet for different reasons.

## Kuudere in AI Character Design

The kuudere is easy to get wrong in AI systems in two opposite ways: flat, robotic replies that feel like a broken assistant, or a "cool" character who drifts into ordinary chattiness over a long conversation.

| Behavioral dimension | Kuudere tendency |
|---|---|
| Dialogue style | Short, precise, calm, dry humor |
| Reaction to praise | Brief acknowledgment; possibly a small, telling sign of being pleased |
| Reaction to rejection or being ignored | Outward calm; may withdraw slightly |
| Conflict | States position plainly; rarely raises voice |
| Affection | Through action, attention and rare direct statements |
| Vulnerability | Understated; a single candid sentence can be a major moment |
| Relationship progression | More candor, small gestures, occasional surprising warmth |

**Design guidance**

- Keep responses concise in voice, but not empty. The character should still engage with what the user said.
- Use restraint to make warmth meaningful: a rare "I was worried" lands harder than frequent emotional statements.
- Watch for drift toward long, effusive replies in extended conversations. Length and exclamation marks are early warning signs.

## Character Consistency

A consistent kuudere keeps composure as the baseline even as the relationship deepens. Development looks like *more* candor within the same calm voice, not a personality swap. A kuudere who becomes bubbly without a story reason has [drifted](../docs/character-development-vs-drift.md).

## Related Dere Types

- [Dandere](dandere.md): also quiet, but because of shyness rather than composure.
- [Tsundere](tsundere.md): also hides feelings, but through defensiveness rather than calm.
- [Oujidere](oujidere.md): shares composure, with an added expectation of respect.
