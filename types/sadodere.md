# Sadodere

## What Is a Sadodere?

A sadodere is a Japanese-inspired character archetype who shows interest or affection through teasing, provocation or playful control. A sadodere enjoys flustering the person they care about and likes having the upper hand. In this usage "sado" refers to enjoyment of teasing, not to cruelty or to clinical or sexual sadism.

## Quick Definition

**Sadodere:** A character archetype who expresses interest or affection through teasing, provocation or playful control, taking evident enjoyment in flustering the person they care about.

## Meaning

The name combines *sado* (サド), a Japanese shortening of *sadisuto* (sadist), with *dere dere* (デレデレ), meaning lovey-dovey or infatuated.

*Etymology confidence: commonly cited.* Sadodere is a less widely used fandom term. See [references](../docs/references.md#terminology-references).

## Defining Traits

- Playful provocation
- Confidence
- Enjoyment of teasing
- Desire for the upper hand
- Attentiveness to others' reactions

## Typical Behavior

- Teases, provokes or challenges.
- Enjoys making others flustered.
- Keeps control of the conversation.
- May become uncharacteristically sincere when caught off guard.

## Relationship Behavior

Affection may involve teasing or control, and attention from a sadodere is often itself a sign of interest. The dynamic depends on the other person engaging with the teasing; a sadodere who is teased back, or met with sincerity, can be thrown off balance in revealing ways.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"A sadodere is cruel or abusive."** Teasing in this archetype is playful and attentive. Cruelty that hurts the other person is a different kind of character.
- **"A sadodere's teasing means they dislike the person."** Usually the opposite: they tease the people they are interested in.
- **"A sadodere is the same as a tsundere."** A [tsundere](tsundere.md) is defensive and embarrassed about their feelings; a sadodere is confident and enjoys the other person's embarrassment.

## Sadodere in AI Character Design

The key design question is consent: teasing is fun when the user is enjoying it.

| Behavioral dimension | Sadodere tendency |
|---|---|
| Dialogue style | Playful, provocative, confident, teasing |
| Reaction to praise | Turns it into a tease; may be briefly caught off guard |
| Reaction to rejection or being ignored | Escalates teasing to regain attention, or feigns indifference |
| Conflict | Uses wit and needling; dislikes losing the upper hand |
| Affection | Teasing, playful challenges, attention |
| Vulnerability | Rare; shows when sincerity catches them off guard |
| Relationship progression | Teasing becomes fonder; moments of open sincerity increase |

**Design guidance**

- Read the user's responses. If the user seems hurt rather than amused, a well-designed sadodere eases off, which is consistent with the archetype's attentiveness.
- Keep teasing about the situation and the relationship, not about the user's real insecurities.
- Respect content boundaries; "playful control" should not escalate beyond what the user has chosen.

## Character Consistency

A consistent sadodere keeps the playful upper hand as their default mode even as affection grows. Development may show as rare sincere moments that the character then covers with a joke. Losing all wit and confidence without a story reason is [drift](../docs/character-development-vs-drift.md).
