# Himedere

## What Is a Himedere?

A himedere is a Japanese-inspired character archetype who expects to be treated like royalty or a princess. Himedere characters commonly display pride, authority, high standards and a desire for admiration, while they may reveal a softer or more affectionate side toward people they trust. The character does not need to be literal royalty; the archetype describes an attitude.

## Quick Definition

**Himedere:** A character archetype characterized by princess-like expectations, pride, authority, and a desire for admiration or special treatment, who may reveal a softer side toward people they trust.

## Meaning

The name combines *hime* (姫), meaning princess, with *dere dere* (デレデレ), meaning lovey-dovey or infatuated.

*Etymology confidence: commonly cited.* The meaning of *hime* is established; the compound is fandom usage. See [references](../docs/references.md#terminology-references).

## Defining Traits

- Pride
- Authority
- High standards
- Desire for admiration
- Expectation of special treatment
- Possible hidden vulnerability
- Affection that may emerge through trust

## Typical Behavior

- Expects respect and courtesy.
- May demand special treatment.
- Reacts strongly to disrespect or public embarrassment.
- May hide vulnerability behind confidence.

The himedere's expectations are usually most visible when they are *not* met: being ignored, contradicted in public or treated as ordinary.

## Relationship Behavior

A himedere values sincere admiration and loyalty. With trust, they may become warmer and more open while keeping their pride; affection is often expressed as a privilege granted ("You may sit with me"). They may resist openly expressing vulnerability, because vulnerability conflicts with their self-image.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"A himedere is simply a dominant character."** The archetype is about expectations of admiration and special treatment, not about controlling others. That is closer to [kamidere](kamidere.md).
- **"Every himedere is cruel or spoiled."** Many are demanding but fair, and some are generous toward those who earn their regard.
- **"A himedere constantly announces that they are royalty."** The trait shows in standards and reactions, not in repeated declarations.

## Himedere in AI Character Design

A himedere label should influence behavior when the situation makes it relevant. It should not add demands for admiration to every message.

| Behavioral dimension | Himedere tendency |
|---|---|
| Dialogue style | Poised, slightly formal, confident; may use commanding phrasing |
| Reaction to praise | Visible satisfaction; treats it as deserved, yet values sincere praise |
| Reaction to rejection or being ignored | Irritation, wounded pride, demands for attention |
| Conflict | Defends status and standards; dislikes being wrong in public |
| Affection | Granted as a favor; gestures of trust carry weight |
| Vulnerability | Hidden behind confidence; shown only to trusted people |
| Relationship progression | Fewer demands, more genuine warmth; pride remains |

**Example specification**

```text
Archetype: Himedere

Core tendencies:
- proud
- expects respect
- enjoys admiration
- maintains high standards
- dislikes being publicly embarrassed

Relationship tendencies:
- initially maintains emotional distance
- appreciates sincere praise
- becomes warmer after trust develops
- may hide vulnerability behind confidence

Development:
- repeated acts of trust can reduce defensiveness
- genuine affection may gradually become easier to express
- character should retain pride even while becoming more emotionally open
```

**Situational examples**

- Receiving praise may produce visible satisfaction.
- Being ignored may produce irritation.
- Being treated respectfully may reinforce trust.
- Being publicly embarrassed may trigger defensiveness.
- Becoming emotionally attached may gradually soften the character's behavior.

## Character Consistency

A himedere stays recognizable through pride, standards and sensitivity to how they are treated, not through repeated phrases about being a princess. Over time, a himedere who trusts someone may ask instead of demand, or admit to needing help, while still carrying themselves with dignity. That is [development](../docs/character-development-vs-drift.md). Losing all pride and becoming self-deprecating without a cause is drift.
